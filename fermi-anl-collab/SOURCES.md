# Sources and provenance

## Product source

The owner specified the Dearly Departed concept, three modules, casket/S3 archive, LDAP access, organizational hierarchy and policy flexibility, lean prototype scope, topic meetings with humans and virtual employees, and the critical importance of explaining why decisions changed over time.

The agreed backlog was exported on 2026-10-28 from the Dearly Departed Agile Prototype Backlog (a private planning page). `USER_STORIES.md` contains the full backlog, with one explicitly noted clarification of the temporal acceptance wording under D05. This ZIP works without access to that Page.

`DECISIONS.md` makes explicit the prototype defaults selected while preparing the handoff. The codebase was not inspected. Fixtures and acceptance expectations are original fictional material; they describe intended behavior, not findings about a real employee or system.

## Agile references

These primary sources inform the documentation structure. They do not prescribe the product's technical architecture.

- [Agile Alliance — Three Cs](https://agilealliance.org/glossary/three-cs/): use a concise story card, a focused conversation about scope, and confirmation of the observable outcome.
- [Bill Wake — INVEST in Good Stories, and SMART Tasks](https://xp123.com/invest-in-good-stories-and-smart-tasks/): assess independence, negotiability, value, estimability, size, and testability; keep delivery slices useful across the necessary layers.
- [Agile Alliance — Given–When–Then](https://agilealliance.org/glossary/given-when-then/): express the starting context, action, and observable result; this form does not require a particular testing tool.
- [The Scrum Guide, November 2020](https://scrumguides.org/scrum-guide.html): refine the backlog, let the implementing developers size work, and use a shared Definition of Done for increments.

References checked on 2026-10-28. No external source establishes that this demo has a particular implementation, performance level, security property, or integration.
